Import your blog articles

Hashnode now charges for API access, so articles are imported once into your own site and served from there. This runs in your browser, where Hashnode loads normally.

This is the site the articles get saved to. Keep it as-is unless you know what you're doing.

Save article to my site

Drag this button onto your bookmarks bar.

How to use

  1. Drag the Save article to my site button above into your browser's bookmarks bar.
  2. Open one of your Hashnode articles below (each opens in a new tab).
  3. On the article page, click the bookmark you just added.
  4. You'll see a confirmation. Repeat for each article. Done — refresh your blog.
